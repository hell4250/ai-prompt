Important Note: The doodles must be drawn simply, using only outlines. Avoid filling areas with color or obscuring large sections of the chart; the candlesticks must remain clearly visible at all times. While the lines may have a playful or lighthearted feel, they must never hide the actual price bars (candlesticks) beneath them. If you draw an animal or a human figure, please make sure to include details like eyes, a nose, and a mouth.
